Who We Are

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla in Chhattisgarh, the InAmigos Foundation has expanded into a nationwide movement. We believe that sustainable development is only possible when all sectors of society are empowered and active participants in growth.

As an ISO 9001:2015 certified Section 8 non-profit holding 80G, 12A, and NITI Aayog registrations, we combine corporate professionalism with grassroots passion to ensure every contribution translates to real-world change.

Certified Transparency

Equipped with 80G & 12A compliance, maintaining absolute transparency for all donations.

Vibrant Youth Network

Mobilizing a massive base of over 30,000+ certified student interns and active volunteers.

Our Ongoing Projects

We run multiple structured initiatives aimed at addressing specific developmental and ecological challenges.

Project SEVA

Focused on community nutrition and basic welfare. We regularly distribute fresh, warm meals and clothing items to underprivileged individuals living in slums and shelter homes.

50,000+ Meals & Clothes Distributed

Project BACHPANSHALA

Bridging educational disparities by setting up localized learning clusters. We teach children foundational concepts, digital literacy, and essential life skills to build confidence.

1,200+ Children Mainstreamed

Project JEEV

Advocating for street animal safety and health. Volunteers organize daily feeding drives, medical rescue support for injured strays, and reflective collar campaigns for highway safety.

8,000+ Animals Fed & Protected

Project UDAAN

Empowering rural and semi-urban women by offering training in stitching, crafts, and financial literacy. We also host hygiene drives to distribute biodegradable sanitary napkins.

5,000+ Women Enabled & Trained

Project PRAKRITI

Restoring green cover and advocating climate action. We run seed-ball throwing activities, sapling plantation drives, and support local farming cooperatives with eco-friendly solutions.

20,000+ Saplings Planted

Project VIKAS

Empowering students through internships in digital operations, graphic design, social media marketing, and content writing, helping them cultivate project-management skills.

30,000+ Interns Successfully Coached
